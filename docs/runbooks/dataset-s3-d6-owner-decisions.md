# Dataset S3 D6 owner decisions (OPEN — not chosen by engineering)

Status: **every decision below is open.** The
[D6 proof](dataset-s3-d6-proof.md) may not start until each row has the
owner's answer and the session is authorized in writing. Each item gives the
options and an engineering recommendation; the recommendation is not a
decision.

E11 to E15 are **required before D6**. P00-02 refuses to start without
E11 = `identity-center` and an E15 gate record, tracked in the reviewed commit,
that records a dedicated proof account (E14). P01-01 and P01-03 check the
account, P01-02 checks every permission set against its template and the
boundary, and P03-11 checks E12. **D6 stays blocked until the owner has
explicitly approved the dedicated account and the identities in it.**

Answers that are identifiers or contact details (E4's recipient, E5, E8, E9,
E12, E14's account lists) are given to the operator running
the proof and are **never committed** to this repository.

| Id | Decision | Options | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|
| **E1** | CloudTrail audit retention (`<AUDIT_RETENTION_DAYS>`) | (a) 90 days, the proof only; (b) 400 days, covering a year of access review plus a margin; (c) a period set by O-3/O-5 legal retention | **(a) for the proof**, with the bucket's default retention raised to the O-3/O-5 value before any customer object is stored. Retention can be lengthened later but, in COMPLIANCE mode, never shortened. |
| **E2** | Object Lock mode on the audit bucket | (a) GOVERNANCE: the audit administrator can shorten retention with `s3:BypassGovernanceRetention`; (b) COMPLIANCE: nobody, including the account root, can delete a log or shorten retention before it ends | **(a) GOVERNANCE for the proof.** COMPLIANCE makes every proof log, and the bucket itself, undeletable for E1's full period. Choose COMPLIANCE for the long-lived staging and production audit buckets, once E1 is final. |
| **E3** | Proof resources after the proof | (a) delete: X06 and X07 remove the dataset bucket, runtime roles, dataset key and trail; (b) retain them in the proof account for later inspection | **(a) delete**, once the evidence is exported. The proof runs in a dedicated account (E14), so its resources can never become the staging deployment; staging is provisioned separately, in its own account, from the same reviewed templates. Choose (b) only to inspect a failure. The proof instance and objects are always removed; the audit bucket, audit key and audit roles are always retained until their retention ends. |
| **E4** | Monthly cost ceiling and its alert | (a) USD 10 above the account's current monthly baseline; (b) USD 25 above it; (c) another figure; plus the alert recipient | **(b) the current baseline plus USD 25.** P03-00 creates an AWS Budgets alert at 80% of that figure and verifies it before any other resource. The budget is account-wide (a tag-filtered budget would need a cost-allocation tag activated first), so its limit must include the existing staging spend. A budget **alerts; it does not stop spending.** The proof's own increment is a few dollars a month (two KMS keys, a small trail, near-empty buckets) plus the proof day's instance hours and requests; confirm prices at provisioning time. |
| **E5** | Approved AWS account id | the dedicated proof account (E14) | **The dedicated proof account only.** It must be none of the staging or production accounts; P00-02, P01-01 and P01-03 refuse anything else. |
| **E6** | Bucket naming in the proof account | The proof always creates `nlw-staging-datasets-<ACCOUNT>-us-east-1-d6<12 random hex>`, so it can never adopt a bucket that already exists (P03-06) | **Keep the suffixed pattern.** The permission sets and the boundary allow only buckets matching it. The application accepts it (`-[a-z0-9]{1,16}`). The staging audit bucket name is exactly 63 characters, the S3 maximum; the same pattern with `production` would be 66 and invalid, so the production names need a shorter form in the production provisioning review. |
| **E7** | A management-event trail exists in the proof account | (a) yes, multi-region, protected; (b) yes, but unprotected or single-region; (c) no | Confirm before P03 from P02's `p02-existing-trails.txt`. **If (b) or (c), stop:** bucket-policy, key-policy and IAM changes would go unrecorded. Creating that trail is an account-level change by the account's administrator, not by the proof. |
| **E8** | `<ADMIN_ROLE>`: the role that provisions and administers the dataset bucket and key | the proof-administrator Identity Center permission set in the proof account, with exactly the `permission-set-proof-admin` inline policy, the proof deny policy and the proof boundary | **Required for D6.** Give it as the **role name with its path** (for example `aws-reserved/sso.amazonaws.com/AWSReservedSSO_NlwD6ProofAdmin_0123456789abcdef`), never as an ARN: the templates build `arn:aws:iam::<ACCOUNT>:role/<ADMIN_ROLE>`. `lib.sh` refuses an ARN, and refuses any `nlw-*-dataset-*` role, because the administrator receives full key administration (S1). P01-02 refuses it unless its policies and boundary are exactly the reviewed ones. |
| **E9** | Which people hold the four permission sets | proof administrator and operator; audit administrator; audit reader | **The operator holds the proof-administrator and operator sets; a different person holds the audit-administrator set; the audit reader is that person or a third.** The lists are permission-set role ARNs; no operator-side role may appear in an audit list (`validate_principals`). The permission-set policies and the boundary then stop the operator from changing the audit roles, trail, audit bucket or audit key, and stop the audit administrator from changing the dataset roles, bucket or key ([provisioning §3.7](dataset-s3-provisioning.md)). **That separation holds only when different approved people hold the two sides and whoever administers the account and Identity Center is neither of them.** The account root, the organization's management account and Identity Center administrators can override it; they are residual authority. |
| **E10** | Where the proof runs | (a) a disposable proof instance (P03-10) in the default VPC; (b) the staging host itself | **(a) the disposable instance.** It needs no change to the staging host, and the proof can be torn down without touching it. Steps P14 and P15 are repeated on the staging host when the refresher ships (O-6). If the account has no default VPC, the owner names a subnet instead. |
| **E11** | Human credential source | IAM Identity Center (short-lived), or IAM users with long-term access keys | **Identity Center only; required before D6.** No long-term access key may be created or used for D6, and `trust-human` refuses every principal that is not an Identity Center permission-set role. Identity Center sessions reach the human roles by role chaining, where `aws:MultiFactorAuthPresent` is generally absent, so **no IAM policy here enforces MFA**: whether sign-in needs MFA is an Identity Center setting, which the verifier attests in the gate record (E15) and nothing checks. Choose a session duration of one hour or less for the four permission sets. |
| **E12** | Who else in the proof account may pass the bootstrap role (`iam:PassRole`) and launch or attach an instance with it | (a) nobody but `<ADMIN_ROLE>`, enforced by the account's administrator; (b) `<ADMIN_ROLE>` and named break-glass principals | **(a).** Anyone who can launch an instance with the bootstrap profile obtains API and ingest credentials. The six dataset and audit roles deny these actions explicitly (`NeverPassOrAttachDatasetRoles`), the proof boundary lets only `<ADMIN_ROLE>` pass the bootstrap role and only to EC2, and P03-11 simulates it. List in `E12_MUST_NOT_PASS_JSON` any other principal of the proof account; P03-11 checks each. |
| **E13** | Bucket-level list calls (`ListBucket`, `ListBucketVersions`) may be missing from the data-event trail | (a) accept: object-level events are recorded, and listings are made only by the operator role; (b) require them: a reviewed selector change and a re-proof | **Decide after P13 reports `p13-list-events.txt`.** If the count is non-zero, nothing is needed. If it is zero, (a) is reasonable for staging, but the gap must stay a documented residual risk until (b) is done for production. |
| **E14** | Where D6 runs | a dedicated AWS proof account, distinct from every staging and production account and holding no production resource | **Decided: a dedicated proof account is required.** The shared-account mode is removed. The owner names the account and lists every staging and production account id (`STAGING_ACCOUNT_IDS_JSON`, `PRODUCTION_ACCOUNT_IDS_JSON`). P00-02 refuses a gate record that is not `dedicated`, names another account, or lists the proof account among them; P01-01 and P01-03 check the caller's account, record its organization and management account against the gate record, and refuse a production-named or production-tagged resource. The account, not the boundary, is the blast-radius boundary; the boundary and the identity policies are additional controls inside it. |
| **E15** | Independent verification before D6 | a named verifier, who is not the operator, checks in the proof account: the account, its organization and management account, and that it holds no production resource; the four permission sets, their role ARNs, that each has exactly its reviewed inline policy, the proof deny policy as its only managed policy, and the proof boundary, and what that allows (simulation and review); that Identity Center requires MFA; and that the account's administrators are not the operator. The verifier records it in `docs/evidence/o2-d6/d6-gate.json`, naming the procedure commit reviewed, in a separately reviewed commit that changes only that file | **Required; D6 is blocked until then.** P00-02 reads the record with `git show` from the reviewed commit, requires that commit's parent to be the procedure commit the record names and the record to be the only change, and refuses any mismatch with the session. It cannot check who wrote the record: the verifier's independence rests on the reviewed pull request that adds it. |

**Sign-off:** owner, date, and the authorized proof window.
